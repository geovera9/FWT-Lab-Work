Website Design Analysis — Bajkamal Singh

Website: https://bajkamalsingh.me/
Document type: UI/UX and visual design analysis

1. Overview

The website is an experimental, personality-driven creative portfolio that combines editorial typography, digital-interface aesthetics, storytelling, and interactive project presentation.

Rather than following a conventional résumé or portfolio template, it aims to create an immersive digital experience that communicates the creator's personality, creative philosophy, and professional capabilities.

Core design principle: Design should demonstrate creativity, not merely describe it.

2. Visual Design and Art Direction
2.1 Typography-Led Identity

The headline, "Creative by night, more creative by midnight," establishes a distinctive personality.

Key characteristics:

Oversized and expressive typography.
Intentional line breaks and contrasting text sizes.
Strong typographic hierarchy.
Playful language that communicates personality.
Typography used as a primary visual element rather than simply for readability.

Strengths

Creates an immediate visual impression.
Establishes a recognizable creative identity.
Makes the portfolio feel personal and memorable.

Potential weaknesses

Excessive typographic experimentation can reduce readability.
Too many competing visual elements may weaken hierarchy.
2.2 Technical, System-Inspired Visual Language

Interface-inspired labels such as SYS.TRACK_ACTIVE, VOL.2026, and System_Active contribute to the site's experimental aesthetic.

Design characteristics:

Technical microcopy.
System-status-inspired labels.
Interface-like section markers.
A combination of expressive display typography and restrained supporting text.

Strengths

Creates a cohesive visual world.
Reinforces the digital and experimental character of the portfolio.
Differentiates the site from conventional personal websites.

Potential weaknesses

Decorative labels may create unnecessary visual noise.
Technical elements should not obscure navigation or content hierarchy.
2.3 Intentional Imperfection

The design philosophy embraces misalignment, asymmetry, and controlled visual disruption.

Key principles:

Asymmetrical composition.
Nontraditional layouts.
Intentional visual tension.
Creative freedom balanced with usability.

Recommendation: Preserve the unconventional art direction while maintaining consistent interaction patterns, alignment cues, and responsive behavior.

4. UX and Interaction Design
4.1 Exploration-Based Interaction

The website uses interaction cues such as:

"Skip Animation"
"Click to expand"
"Drag your cursor to explore"
Hover-based project exploration

These patterns encourage visitors to discover the interface rather than simply read static content.

Strengths

Makes the experience memorable.
Encourages engagement.
Reinforces the experimental design language.

Risks

Interactive elements may not be immediately discoverable.
Hover-dependent behavior may not translate well to touchscreens.
Excessive motion can distract from the content.
4.2 Scroll-Driven Storytelling

The page progression creates a chapter-like experience, taking visitors from personal identity through achievements and projects.

Recommendation: Use animation to reinforce meaningful transitions rather than animate every element independently.

4.3 Accessibility and User Control

The animation-skip option is a positive usability decision.

Further improvements should include:

Support for prefers-reduced-motion.
Full keyboard navigation.
Visible focus indicators.
Accessible labels for interactive controls.
Adequate color contrast.
Touch alternatives for hover interactions.
Clear interaction states.

Recommendations

The problem being solved.
The target audience.

This would make the portfolio more useful to recruiters, clients, and creative collaborators.

6. Featured Project: Delhi Metro Concept

The featured project uses the Hindi headline "दिल्ली मेट्रो में आपका स्वागत है" ("Welcome to Delhi Metro"), with an "ENTER METRO" call to action and Blue Line-themed supporting copy.

The website identifies creative disciplines including typography, motion graphics, social media, and visual identity.

Design Strengths
Conceptual consistency: The metro theme creates a recognizable organizing idea.
Cultural context: Hindi language and New Delhi references establish a sense of place.
Multidisciplinary scope: Multiple listed disciplines suggest a broader creative system.
Interactive presentation: The invitation to enter the project encourages exploration.
Recommendations

Develop the project into a complete case study featuring:

Visual identity.
Typography and color palette.
Graphic assets and compositions.
Motion design examples.
Digital and social media mockups.
Design rationale.
Project objectives and outcomes.

Clearly distinguish conceptual projects from commissioned client work where applicable.

9. Priority Improvements
Priority 1: Make Selected Work Immediately Accessible

Add a clear "View Selected Work" link in the opening viewport.

Preserve the immersive introduction, but allow recruiters and potential clients to bypass it.

Priority 2: Create Detailed Project Case Studies

Show the brief, strategy, design decisions, personal contribution, deliverables, and results.

This communicates strategic thinking alongside visual execution.

Priority 3: Make Motion Optional

Keep the expressive animation while ensuring the experience works without it.

Test reduced-motion preferences, keyboard interaction, and touch behavior.

Priority 4: Optimize the Mobile Experience

Review:

Headline wrapping.
Spacing and layout.
Image crops.
Tap target sizes.
Technical microcopy readability.
Animation performance.
Navigation and project discovery.

Avoid simply compressing the desktop layout into a smaller viewport.

Priority 5: Clarify the Professional Offering

Explain who the creator works with, what creative services they offer, and how visitors can initiate a collaboration.

The professional offering should complement the personality-driven identity rather than replace it.

11. Final Verdict

The website's greatest strength is its ability to express the creator's personality and creative philosophy through the design itself.

Its experimental typography, system-inspired interface labels, personal narrative, quantitative achievements, and themed project presentation reinforce one another to create a distinctive portfolio.

The main opportunity is not to make the website more visually complicated. It is to make the existing creativity work harder.

The most valuable improvements are:

Give visitors faster access to the strongest projects.
Explain the thinking and process behind the work.
Improve mobile usability and accessibility.
Make the professional offering more explicit.
Measure actual performance and usability before making further changes.

Final recommendation: Keep the art direction, reduce unnecessary friction, and give the work more room to prove its value.
